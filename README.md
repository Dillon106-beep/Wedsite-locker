# Project Overview
Dillon's Bakery website is a responsive site designed to showcase its cakes, bread and pastries and allow customers to easily view and order online, Homepage, about us, Menu, Order now and contact warm clean colors and consistent product cards built using HTML and CSS to boost its online presence and simply ordering.

# Goals and Objectives
The goal of Dillon's Bakery website is to create n attractive, responsive site that promotes the bakery and makes ordering easy, with objectives to showcase products with images and prices, provide simple navigation, allow online orders and contact, maintain a consistent brand look using CSS and ensure the site is mobile friendly for all customers.

# Website Features and Funtionality
Homepage: Serves a the first impression with the business logo and the contact us button to start or process your order. It highlights the best selling products, daily specials and customer testimonials to attract visitors.

About us: Shares the story behind Dillon's Bakery when it started its mission and commitment to fresh ingredient. 

Menu: Displays all baked goods n organized categories like cakes, bread, pastries and muffins. each itt item is shown as a product card with an image, name price styled consistently using CSS.

Order now: With ordering customers will select what they want to order and add it to the cart and pay by logging in their personal information and banking details to make the transaction, then there will be delivery services that will get your baked goods to your house, workplace or where ever.

Contact: There will be the bakery's contact details for orders, any complaints on the business, service received from the employees and comments on the food and drinks
